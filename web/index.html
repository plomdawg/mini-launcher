▧Cover artworkFront and back art, fit to the case face⌄

LaunchBox Games DB has scanned box fronts, backs and spines by region (no key needed). 600 × 900 SteamGridDB grids are 2:3, a little narrower than a DVD insert face (129.5 × 183 mm). Fill crops a few mm off the top and bottom (use crop position to keep a logo); Fit + blurred extension keeps the whole image and fills the sides with a blurred copy. Spine art (e.g. a scan of the original spine) replaces the spine title and logo and covers the spine fill (with Fit whole image, the fill shows around it); landscape images are rotated to match the spine direction. Drop an image on any tile or browse. Artwork stays in this browser unless you export a project file.
